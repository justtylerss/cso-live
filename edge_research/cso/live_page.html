How to read this map

Lines
Each line is a POI on your chart. Green = above price, red = below. Solid = a kind tested on its own history; dashed = untested kind (its odds come from its distance only). Faded = already traded through this session.
The big %
On past NQ days (2024-26) in the same spot at this clock time, how often price reached that level before the end of the current session. The smaller numbers are later windows (by 4 AM, by 8:29 AM, by 3 PM).
Usually ~time
The median time it was reached, on the days it was, shown as tonight's clock time.
n
How many past days the % rests on. Under 15 is thin (faded on the list); under 30, treat lightly.
Any price this far
The placebo: the same odds for a random price at the same distance, on days with a similar daily range. If the POI's % isn't clearly above it, the level adds nothing beyond its distance.
Through / back
After price touched the level: how often it went 20 pts through first vs came 20 pts back first (within 60 min).
11% → 24%
The % changed since the last read (price moved, time passed, or a level was touched).
What it isn't
Descriptive odds from history. Not a signal, not a prediction, not advice.

If this happens

Conditional odds that open up if price does something at a nearby POI. Shown only when 65%+ on 30+ past days and at least 5 pts above the odds from right now. A row glows when its trigger happens live.
